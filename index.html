The Atelier & Philosophy

Where Precision Meets High Fashion

"Every nail is a miniature bespoke canvas. We reject rushed appointments in favor of architectural precision, flawless cuticle geometry, and durable luxury."

Nestled in Edinburgh, the private atelier of Aleksandra Bochenek delivers an elevated sanctuary for clients seeking uncompromising nail artistry. Each appointment is an immersive, 1-on-1 bespoke experience dedicated to the health, strength, and aesthetic perfection of your natural nails and extensions.

Specialising in advanced European Russian e-file dry manicure techniques, Aleksandra creates seamless cuticle lines that allow polish to grow out invisibly, guaranteeing weeks of pristine wear without lifting or chipping.

  • Hospital-Grade Sterilization

    Autoclave-sterilized tools and single-use files for every guest.

  • European Dry E-File

    Micro-precision cuticle detailing for weeks of clean grow-out.

  • Hypoallergenic Luxury Gels

    Premium European builder gels, free from harsh monomers.

  • Custom Haute Artistry

    Hand-painted calligraphy, gold foil accents, and ombre mastery.
